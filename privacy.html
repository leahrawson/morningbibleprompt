Privacy.

Morning is designed so your reflections can remain private to the browser you use.

Your writing

Text you enter into Morning is stored locally in your browser so it can remain available while you write. Morning does not include an account system, database, or feature that sends your reflections to the site owner.

When you choose Begin the day, Morning removes that day’s reflection text from its local browser storage.

Local storage

Morning uses your browser’s local storage to remember the current day’s writing and prompt selection. This information stays with that browser profile unless you clear browser data, use private browsing, change devices or browsers, or the browser removes stored site data.

Third-party services

Morning currently loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. That request is made by your browser to Google when the page loads. The site does not currently include advertising, behavioral tracking, session replay, or a third-party analytics service.

Hosting

If Morning is hosted with GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical information associated with requests to the site as part of operating and securing its service. Morning itself does not use that information to identify visitors.

Changes

If Morning later adds accounts, cloud syncing, analytics, or other services that change how information is handled, this page should be updated before those features are released.
